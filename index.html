About

I'm an AI engineer. My start was search, ranking and evaluation at Microsoft, which taught me that the hard part is rarely the model. It's knowing whether a change actually helped.

I graduated top of my class in Computer Science at the University of Nairobi, and was a 2023 Deep Learning Indaba scholar in Accra.

Failures usually live upstream of the ranker: in how documents were modelled, what was indexed, and whether the query was ever a search query.

I speak English and Swahili fluently, and some French. Now I build LLM apps, retrieval and agents end to end.
